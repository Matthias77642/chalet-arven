About Wengen

An alpine village of timeless charm

Car-free, scenic, and beautifully placed beneath the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau. For visitor information, see the official Wengen tourist office website: https://wengen.swiss/en/ .

Wengen is a picturesque, car-free alpine village set on a sunny terrace at 1,274 metres in the heart of the UNESCO Jungfrau-Aletsch World Heritage Site. Surrounded by the famous Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau, it is one of Switzerland’s most attractive mountain resorts, with traditional chalets, welcoming hotels, and no high-rise buildings.

The village is a superb destination in every season. In winter, it offers excellent skiing, snowboarding, and ski touring; in summer, it becomes a base for hiking, trekking, mountain biking, and simply enjoying the crisp mountain air and extraordinary views.

Once a farming community, Wengen became a sought-after resort in the 19th century and remains famous today for the celebrated Lauberhorn ski race, one of the great events of the World Cup calendar.

Chalet Arven

Historic character with modern alpine comfort

A stylish apartment blending the charm of one of Wengen’s oldest chalets with a calm, contemporary interior.

Chalet Arven is a beautifully renovated three-bedroom apartment designed for relaxed, comfortable stays in the mountains. Set in a prime central position just off the main street, it is only a three-minute walk from both the train station and the Männlichen cable car.

The apartment sleeps up to six guests and includes a bright open-plan living, dining, and kitchen area, two bathrooms, and a large sunny terrace with exceptional views of the Jungfrau and Silberhorn mountains.

A well-equipped kitchen, shared laundry and storage space, a garden area, and a ski room make Chalet Arven an easy and inviting base throughout the year.
